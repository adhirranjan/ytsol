Concept In the general circle x2+y2+2gx+2fy+c=0 the number c is the constant (absolute) term. A curve passes through a point only if that point's coordinates satisfy the equation, so testing the origin isolates c.
Formula value at (0,0) = c. putting x=0, y=0 kills every x- and y-term, leaving only c.
Solution
1. Write the general circle x2+y2+2gx+2fy+c=0. standard second-degree form with equal x2,y2 coefficients.
2. The origin lies on the circle, so (0,0) must satisfy it.
3. Substitute: 0+0+0+0+c=0 ⇒ c=0. all variable terms vanish at the origin.
4. Hence the constant term equals zero whenever the circle passes through the origin.
5. Geometrically c is the power of the origin w.r.t. the circle; a point on the circle has zero power.
6. So the answer is c = 0, i.e. Zero.
