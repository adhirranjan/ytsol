10The product of all values of x which make (log₃ x)(log₅ 9) − log_x 25 + log₃ 2 = log₃ 54 true, is: (a) √5 (b) 5 (c) 5√5 (d) 25Product of roots with mixed log basesProduct of all values of x satisfying a log eqn🟡 Similarc5√5

2. Right side: log₃ 54 = log₃(27 × 2) = 3 + log₃ 2.Product rule; 54 = 27·2 and log₃27 = 3.
3. Cancel log₃ 2 from both sides: (log₃ x)(log₅ 9) − log_x 25 = 3.The lone log₃2 on the left was placed there purely to be cancelled.
4. First term: (log₃x)(log₅9) = (ln x/ln 3)(ln 9/ln 5) = (ln x/ln 3)(2 ln 3/ln 5) = 2 ln x/ln 5 = 2 log₅ x.ln 9 = 2 ln 3 cancels the ln 3 in the denominator.
5. Second term: log_x 25 = log_x 5² = 2 log_x 5 = 2/(log₅ x).Power rule then the reciprocal rule.
6. Let m = log₅ x (m ≠ 0). The equation becomes 2m − 2/m = 3.One unknown at last.
