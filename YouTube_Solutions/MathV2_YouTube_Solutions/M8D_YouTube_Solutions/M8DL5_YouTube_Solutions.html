Concept Two facts carry this whole question, and both are worth burning into memory. Inserting means never changes the total: the sum of n arithmetic means between a and b equals n times the single A.M., and the product of n geometric means between a and b equals the nth power of the single G.M. Nothing needs to be computed term by term.
Formula A₁ + A₂ + … + Aₙ = n(a + b)/2 · G₁G₂ … Gₙ = (ab)^(n/2). For n = 2 these read A₁ + A₂ = a + b and G₁G₂ = ab.
Solution
1. Two A.M.'s means a, A₁, A₂, b is an A.P. of 4 terms, so b = a + 3d ⇒ d = (b − a)/3.
2. A₁ = a + d and A₂ = a + 2d, so A₁ + A₂ = 2a + 3d = 2a + (b − a) = a + b.The two inner terms are symmetric about the mid-point, so together they reproduce the two outer terms.
3. Two G.M.'s means a, G₁, G₂, b is a G.P. of 4 terms, so b = ar³ ⇒ r³ = b/a.
4. G₁ = ar and G₂ = ar², so G₁G₂ = a²r³ = a²·(b/a) = ab.
5. Therefore (A₁ + A₂)/(G₁G₂) = (a + b)/(ab).Which is also 1/a + 1/b — i.e. twice the reciprocal of the harmonic mean.
6. Numeric check with a = 2, b = 4: A₁ = 8/3, A₂ = 10/3, sum = 6 = a + b ✓; r³ = 2 so G₁G₂ = a²r³ = 4·2 = 8 = ab ✓; ratio = 6/8 = 3/4, and (a+b)/ab = 6/8 = 3/4 ✓
  a , A1 , A2 , b   (A.P.)  ->  A1 + A2 = a + b
  a , G1 , G2 , b   (G.P.)  ->  G1 * G2 = a * b

  (A1 + A2) / (G1 G2) = (a + b) / (ab)  =  1/a + 1/b

Answer: (a + b)/ab.

2. Substitute the given values: 2² = 4 × H.

9. Reject (a) and (b): both assert A and G differ, which the algebra refutes for every a.
   x^2 - 2ax + b  = 0   ->  sum     of roots = 2a   ->  A = a
   x^2 - 2bx + a^2 = 0  ->  product of roots = a^2  ->  G = a

   A = G                       (b is irrelevant)

   a = 5 , b = 4 :  A = 5 , G = sqrt25 = 5     ok
⚡ JEE Trick A.M. of roots = −(coefficient of x)/(2 × coefficient of x²); G.M. of roots = √(constant/leading). Once those are second nature, questions like this take ten seconds. Beware of importing A ≥ G here — that chain applies to the two means of the same pair, whereas A and G here come from two different equations, so equality is perfectly legitimate.
Answer: (c) A = G (both equal a).
7If the A.M. and G.M. of roots of a quadratic equation are 8 and 5 respectively, then the quadratic equation will be
(a) x² − 16x − 25 = 0   (b) x² − 8x + 5 = 0   (c) x² − 16x + 25 = 0   (d) x² + 16x − 25 = 0		Building a quadratic from A and GIf AM and GM of roots of a quadratic equation are 8 and 5 respectively, then obtain the quadratic.🟢 Exactcx² − 16x + 25 = 0
Concept The reverse of the previous question: given the two means, reconstruct the equation. A gives the sum (double it), G gives the product (square it), and the standard template x² − (sum)x + (product) = 0 assembles the answer. This is NCERT Class 11, Exercise 9.3 — a guaranteed-format question.
Formula sum = 2A, product = G² ⇒ x² − 2Ax + G² = 0. Memorise this compact template; it directly encodes both means.
Solution
1. A.M. of the roots = 8 ⇒ sum of the roots = 2 × 8 = 16.
2. G.M. of the roots = 5 ⇒ product of the roots = 5² = 25.
3. Standard form: x² − (sum)x + (product) = 0 ⇒ x² − 16x + 25 = 0 ⇒ option (c).
4. Check via the compact template x² − 2Ax + G² = 0 with A = 8, G = 5: x² − 16x + 25 = 0 ✓ same thing.
5. Verify by solving: discriminant = 256 − 100 = 156, √156 = 2√39 ≈ 12.49, so roots = (16 ± 12.49)/2 ≈ 14.245 and 1.755.
6. Their A.M. = (14.245 + 1.755)/2 = 8 ✓ and their G.M. = √(14.245 × 1.755) = √25.0 = 5 ✓
7. Reject the distractors: (a) and (d) have product −25 (wrong sign), and (d) also has sum −16; (b) uses the means themselves (8 and 5) as the coefficients instead of 2A and G².
8. Consistency: A = 8 ≥ G = 5 ✓, so real roots exist — as the positive discriminant confirms.
   sum     = 2A  = 16
   product = G^2 = 25

   x^2 - (sum) x + (product) = 0   ->   x^2 - 16x + 25 = 0

   roots ~ 14.245 , 1.755    A.M. = 8 , G.M. = 5     ok
⚡ JEE Trick Burn in the template x² − 2Ax + G² = 0. Then check the sign discipline: the coefficient of x is negative when the sum is positive, and the constant term is positive because G² is a square — that pair of observations alone eliminates (a), (b) and (d) here without any arithmetic.
Answer: (c) x² − 16x + 25 = 0.
8Passage — Let A1, A2, A3, …, Am be arithmetic means between −2 and 1027 and G1, G2, G3, …, Gn be geometric means between 1 and 1024. Product of geometric means is 245 and sum of arithmetic means is 1025×171. — The value of m is
(a) 340   (b) 342   (c) 344   (d) 346		Sum of m A.M.'s → mArithmetic mean — Insert n arithmetic means between two given number (solved example)🟡 Similarb342
Concept The "sum of m A.M.'s = m × (single A.M.)" shortcut, used in reverse. The single A.M. of −2 and 1027 is 1025/2, and the printed total is deliberately written as 1025 × 171 so that the 1025 cancels and m falls out in one step.
Formula A₁ + A₂ + … + A_m = m(a + b)/2. The m inserted means have the same average as the two endpoints.
Solution
1. The endpoints are a = −2 and b = 1027, so a + b = 1025.
2. Sum of the m arithmetic means = m × 1025/2.
3. Set this equal to the given total: m × 1025/2 = 1025 × 171.
4. Cancel 1025 from both sides: m/2 = 171 ⇒ m = 342 ⇒ option (b).
5. Verify by building the A.P.: with m = 342 inserted terms there are 344 terms total, so 1027 = −2 + 343d ⇒ 343d = 1029 ⇒ d = 3.1029 = 3 × 343, a clean division — strong evidence m = 342 is right.
6. So A_k = −2 + 3k for k = 1, …, 342 (needed again in Q10).
7. Check the sum directly: Σ A_k = Σ(−2 + 3k) = −2(342) + 3 × 342(343)/2 = −684 + 3 × 58653 = −684 + 175959 = 175275. And 1025 × 171 = 175275 ✓
8. Reject the neighbours: m = 340 would give sum 1025 × 170, m = 344 would give 1025 × 172 — the "171" pins m exactly.
   endpoints : -2 and 1027      a + b = 1025

   sum of m A.M.s = m * 1025/2 = 1025 * 171   ->  m/2 = 171  ->  m = 342

   check :  344 terms ,  1027 = -2 + 343d  ->  d = 3
            A_k = -2 + 3k ,  sum = 175275 = 1025 * 171     ok
⚡ JEE Trick When a passage prints a total in factored form like 1025 × 171, that factorisation is the hint — one factor is the endpoint sum and the other is m/2. Also compute d = 3 straight away: the later parts of the passage (Q10 here) almost always need it.
Answer: (b) m = 342 (common difference d = 3, A_k = −2 + 3k).
9Passage (contd.) — The value of G1 + G2 + G3 + … + Gn is
(a) 1024   (b) 2044   (c) 512   (d) 1022		Product of n G.M.'s → n, then the G.P. sumClass 11 Maths — Find n Geometric Mean Between two Numbers, R B Classes🟡 Similard1022
Concept Identical to LEVEL-3 Q1: extract n from the product condition, then sum the resulting geometric progression. The product of n G.M.'s between 1 and 1024 is (1024)^(n/2) = 2^(5n), and setting that to 2⁴⁵ gives n = 9.
Formula G₁G₂⋯Gₙ = (ab)^(n/2) · Sₙ = G₁(rⁿ − 1)/(r − 1). The endpoints 1 and 1024 = 2¹⁰ make every power a clean power of 2.
Solution
1. Here a = 1 and b = 1024 = 2¹⁰, so ab = 2¹⁰.
2. Product of the n G.M.'s = (2¹⁰)^(n/2) = 2^(5n).
3. Given product = 2⁴⁵ ⇒ 5n = 45 ⇒ n = 9.
4. The full G.P. is 1, G₁, …, G₉, 1024 — 11 terms, so r¹⁰ = 1024 = 2¹⁰ ⇒ r = 2.
5. Therefore G_k = 2^k, i.e. G₁ = 2, G₂ = 4, …, G₉ = 512.G_k = 1 × r^k = 2^k — worth recording, since Q10 needs G₅.
6. Sum = 2 + 4 + 8 + … + 512 = 2(2⁹ − 1)/(2 − 1) = 2 × 511 = 1022 ⇒ option (d).
7. Verify the product: G₁⋯G₉ = 2^(1+2+⋯+9) = 2⁴⁵ ✓ exactly as stated in the passage.
8. Direct addition: 2 + 4 + 8 + 16 + 32 + 64 + 128 + 256 + 512 = 1022 ✓
9. Reject (c) 512: that is G₉ alone, not the sum; reject (a) 1024: that is the endpoint; reject (b) 2044: that is twice 1022, i.e. the sum including a spurious extra doubling.
   product of n G.M.s = (1 * 1024)^(n/2) = 2^(5n) = 2^45  ->  n = 9

   11 terms , r = 2 ,  G_k = 2^k

   sum = 2 + 4 + ... + 512 = 2(2^9 - 1) = 1022
⚡ JEE Trick Sums of consecutive powers of 2 are always "next power minus the first term": 2 + 4 + … + 512 = 1024 − 2 = 1022. Recognising that saves the formula entirely. Also record G_k = 2^k now — passage questions are designed so that the work in one part feeds the next.
Answer: (d) 1022 (n = 9, r = 2, G_k = 2^k).
10Passage (contd.) — The numbers 2A171, G5² + 1, 2A172 are in
(a) A.P.   (b) G.P.   (c) H.P.   (d) A.G.P.		Combining the passage's A.P. and G.P.Arithmetic Mean — Insert n Arithmetic Means Between Two Given Number || Lecture-07🟡 SimilaraA.P.
Concept The payoff of the passage: use d = 3 and A_k = −2 + 3k from Q8, and G_k = 2^k from Q9, to evaluate three specific numbers. Then test which progression they form by checking whether the gaps are equal (A.P.) or the ratios are equal (G.P.).
Formula A_k = −2 + 3k (from m = 342, d = 3) · G_k = 2^k (from n = 9, r = 2) · three numbers are in A.P. iff 2×(middle) = (first) + (third). The middle-term test is faster than computing both gaps.
Solution
1. From Q8: the A.P. runs −2, …, 1027 with d = 3, so A_k = −2 + 3k.
2. A₁₇₁ = −2 + 3(171) = −2 + 513 = 511, so 2A₁₇₁ = 1022.
3. A₁₇₂ = −2 + 3(172) = −2 + 516 = 514, so 2A₁₇₂ = 1028.
4. From Q9: G_k = 2^k, so G₅ = 2⁵ = 32.
5. Hence G₅² + 1 = 32² + 1 = 1024 + 1 = 1025.
6. The three numbers are 1022, 1025, 1028.
7. Gaps: 1025 − 1022 = 3 and 1028 − 1025 = 3 — equal, so they are in A.P. ⇒ option (a).
8. Confirm with the middle-term test: 2 × 1025 = 2050, and 1022 + 1028 = 2050 ✓
9. Rule out G.P.: 1025/1022 ≈ 1.00294 while 1028/1025 ≈ 1.00293 — close but not equal, and exactly equal would require 1025² = 1022 × 1028, i.e. 1050625 = 1050616 ✗ (they differ by 9).This near-miss is deliberate: the setter wants you to check exactly, not approximately.
10. Rule out H.P.: that would need the reciprocals in A.P., i.e. 2/1025 = 1/1022 + 1/1028, which fails since the numbers are already in A.P. and are distinct.
   A_k = -2 + 3k         G_k = 2^k

   2 A_171 = 2(-2 + 513) = 1022
   G_5^2+1 = 32^2 + 1    = 1025
   2 A_172 = 2(-2 + 516) = 1028

   gaps : 3 , 3     ->   A.P.
   check: 2(1025) = 2050 = 1022 + 1028      ok
   G.P.? 1025^2 = 1050625  vs  1022*1028 = 1050616    no
⚡ JEE Trick To classify three numbers, always test 2b = a + c for A.P. and b² = ac for G.P. — two multiplications, no division, no rounding. When the numbers are large and close together (as here, 1022/1025/1028), decimal ratios look almost equal, so an exact integer test is essential. Also note 1024 = 2¹⁰ appearing again: passage questions recycle the same constants deliberately.
Answer: (a) A.P. — the numbers are 1022, 1025, 1028 with common difference 3.
