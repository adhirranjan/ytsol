How every quiz works

  1. Choose lectures — all are selected by default, so the whole chapter is quizzed.
  2. Answer and submit. Anything left blank counts as wrong.
  3. Review — compulsory, no skip. Every question you got wrong or left blank is shown with why the correct option is correct and why each other option is wrong, plus full working.
  4. Re-quiz on exactly those questions, with the options reshuffled.
  5. Repeat until everything is correct.
