# RT-5 Chapter Priority (exam 2026-11-16)

**Priority = (weight on a cumulative RT) × (not covered by past-paper repeats) × (how much it feeds other chapters).**

Weights are anchored to the RT-4 measured per-subject quotas. The CAT-6 block is pushed up because on a cumulative RT the newest chapters carry ~40–50% *and* appear in ~1 of 525 past exam questions — so drilling old papers won't cover them.

Syllabus: P1–P11, C1–C6, M1–M9 (no Complex-II).

## Physics

| Tier | Chapters | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — master first** | **P9 Collision · P10 Circular Motion · P11 Centre of Mass** | Newest block (CAT-6), weighted heaviest on an RT, and *zero* past-paper repeat cover — the mocks/notes are your only source |
| | **P8 Work-Energy-Power** | Biggest recurring share in RT-4 (55); collision & circular motion are built on it |
| | **P4 Motion 1D/2D · P6 NLM** | High share (60 / 36) and the backbone every later mechanics chapter leans on |
| **2 — solid** | P7 Friction · P3 Vectors · P5 Force System | Mid-weight; P7 pairs with P8, P3/P5 are setup skills |
| **3 — embedded, don't study standalone** | P2 BMT-2 (calculus) · P1 BMT-1 (coord-geo) | They appear *inside* other problems (differentiate a v(t), etc.), ~8–16 share. Keep the skill sharp; don't spend blocks on them |

## If time runs short, the non-negotiable six

**P9/P10/P11 + C6 + M8D + M9** — the CAT-6 block. On this RT they're worth the most marks and are the *only* part of the syllabus your "re-solve old papers" lever can't touch. Everything in Tier 1 above is the next ring out.
