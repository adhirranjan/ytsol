## Marking scheme reminder (4R−1W)

+4 correct · −1 wrong · 0 blank. Educated 2-option guess EV = (½·+4)+(½·−1) = **+1.5** → worth it.
Blind guess on an unknown topic ≈ 0 → leave it. Your score gap is calibration, not knowledge.
