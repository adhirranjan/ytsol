2 · How the loop works

  1. Answer every question in the round and hit Submit.
  2. You then go to the review — this step is compulsory, there is no skip.
  3. The review shows every question you got wrong or left blank, with why the right option is right and why each wrong option is wrong.
  4. You are then re-quizzed on exactly those questions, options reshuffled.
  5. This repeats until every selected question is correct.
