Concept The extended sine rule says each side divided by the sine of its opposite angle is the same constant, and that constant is the diameter of the circumcircle, 2R. The trap is to write just R, or to forget that the ratio is a length (side over a pure number), so it must be a length like 2R, not a ratio like r/R.

Solution
