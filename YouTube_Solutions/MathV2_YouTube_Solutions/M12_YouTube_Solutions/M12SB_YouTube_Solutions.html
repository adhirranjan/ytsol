 E = -(cosP + cosQ + cosR) = -(1 + r/R)
 r/R <= 1/2  (R >= 2r)
 E >= -(1 + 1/2) = -3/2 , equality: equilateral
 -----------------------------------------
 min E = -3/2                    <-- answer
⚡ JEE Trick Symmetric expression in a triangle: its extreme value is almost always at the equilateral triangle, so evaluate 3 cos 120° = −3/2 and pick (c). Trap: answering 3/2, the MAXIMUM of cos P + cos Q + cos R, forgetting the minus sign from cos(π − R); ±5/3 are pure distractors.
Answer: (c) −3/2.
